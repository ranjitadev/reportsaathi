# Core Flow, Feature Breakdown & Scope (MVP vs. Later vs. Never)

Based on the problem definition in [`docs/01-problem.md`](file:///c:/Users/Ranjitha/reportsaathi/docs/01-problem.md) and the market/regulatory findings in [`docs/02-research.md`](file:///c:/Users/Ranjitha/reportsaathi/docs/02-research.md), this document establishes the single core flow for **ReportSaathi**, breaks it into features, and ruthlessly categorizes them into **MVP**, **Later**, and **Never**.

---

## 1. The Single Core Flow

```
[Patient receives Lab PDF via SMS/WhatsApp]
                   │
                   ▼
       1. Upload Digital PDF Report
                   │
                   ▼
 2. Local PII Redaction & Parameter Extraction
    (Strip Name, Phone, UHID; Extract Biomarkers & Units)
                   │
                   ▼
  3. Patient Verifies Extracted Values (Sanity Check)
                   │
                   ▼
 4. Generate Plain-Language Vernacular Report
    • Biomarker organ & physiological role
    • De-stigmatized, contextual explanation of out-of-range flags
    • Common non-alarming reasons (hydration, diet, timing)
                   │
                   ▼
 5. Generate Pre-Consultation "Doctor Question Checklist"
    (3 high-yield questions tailored for the patient's upcoming OPD visit)
```

**Main Value Delivered**: Converts an intimidating, uninterpreted lab PDF with alarming red flags into an empowering, anxiety-reducing vernacular summary with 3 clear questions for the doctor—in under 60 seconds, with zero regulatory risk and zero data privacy leakage.

## 2. Feature & Sub-Feature Breakdown

### Feature 1: Document Ingestion & Privacy Guard
* **1.1 Digital PDF Upload**: Ingestion of digital vector PDFs generated by labs.
* **1.2 Password Unlocker**: Automated prompt to decrypt PDFs locked with DOB/phone (standard for Indian labs like Dr. Lal / Apollo).
* **1.3 Smartphone Camera Photo / Scan Upload**: Multi-angle image capture and glare-reduction for physical paper reports.
* **1.4 Client-Side / Edge PII De-identification**: Masking patient name, age, phone number, address, and barcode/UHID prior to sending data to processing engines.

### Feature 2: Extraction & Quality Control
* **2.1 Biomarker & Unit Parser**: OCR and table parser extracting Parameter Name, Measured Value, Unit, and Reference Interval.
* **2.2 Extraction Confidence & Review Screen**: A quick 1-tap confirmation screen allowing the user to spot-check extracted values against their original document to prevent OCR hallucination.
* **2.3 Unit Standardization**: Internal mapping engine recognizing common Indian lab formats (e.g., `mg/dL`, `g/dL`, `µIU/mL`, `cells/cu.mm`).

### Feature 3: Plain-Language & Vernacular Explanation Engine
* **3.1 Medical Jargon Translator**: Translating technical names into plain language (e.g., *"SGOT / AST = An enzyme found primarily in liver cells"*).
* **3.2 Contextual "Out-of-Range" Normalizer**: Removing the panic of red asterisks by explaining *why* a number might sit slightly outside a reference range (e.g., fasting state, temporary viral infection, exercise, hydration).
* **3.3 Vernacular Multi-Language Selector**: Toggle between English and Indian regional languages (Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Bengali).
* **3.4 Audio / Voice Read-Aloud**: Text-to-speech audio playback for elderly or low-literacy users.

### Feature 4: Pre-Consultation Doctor Question Builder
* **4.1 Targeted 3-Question Checklist**: Generating 3 specific, medically sound questions for the patient to ask their physician (e.g., *"Doctor, my HbA1c is 6.2%—does this warrant lifestyle modification or immediate medication?"*).
* **4.2 Patient Preparation Brief**: A brief reminder of what the patient should disclose to the doctor (current prescriptions, supplements, fasting duration).
* **4.3 1-Click Shareable Summary Card**: A clean, single-page PDF/image summary ready to show the doctor or share with a caregiver.

### Feature 5: Safety Guardrails & Statutory Boundaries
* **5.1 Red-Flag Clinical Escalation Banner**: Non-alarmist, prominent safety notices for critically dangerous extreme values (e.g., Platelets < 20,000/µL or Potassium > 6.5 mEq/L) advising urgent in-person medical evaluation.
* **5.2 Clear Statutory Disclaimers**: Persistent notification indicating the product provides educational health information, not a medical diagnosis, complying with Telemedicine Practice Guidelines 2020.

### Feature 6: Longitudinal Tracking & Accounts
* **6.1 User Authentication & Family Profiles**: Mobile OTP sign-in with profiles for Self, Mother, Father, Spouse.
* **6.2 Historical Trend Graphing**: Longitudinal tracking of chronic markers (e.g., plotting HbA1c or TSH over multiple months).
* **6.3 ABHA / ABDM Health Locker Integration**: Connecting with Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission to pull records automatically via Consent Managers.

### Feature 7: Ecosystem & Distribution Integrations
* **7.1 WhatsApp Bot Flow**: End-to-end ingestion and delivery directly through a WhatsApp Business number.
* **7.2 Doctor Video Teleconsultation Booking**: In-app booking with a partner doctor.
* **7.3 Pharmacy Medicine Ordering & Test Re-booking**: Links to buy prescription medicines or book follow-up lab tests.

### Feature 8: Clinical Decision Making & Interventions
* **8.1 Autonomous Disease Diagnosis**: Stating definite clinical conditions (e.g., *"You have Chronic Kidney Disease Stage 3"*).
* **8.2 Medication & Prescription Modifications**: Advising patients to alter drug dosages or stop medications.
* **8.3 Unverified Home Remedies & Herbal Cures**: Recommending unproven home concoctions.

## 4. Ruthless MVP Challenge: What Was Cut & Why

To ensure the MVP is truly minimal and laser-focused, multiple seemingly "standard" features were challenged and ejected to **Later**:

1. **Challenged: User Accounts, Logins & Database Storage (Moved to LATER)**
   * *The Assumption*: "Users need an account to save their reports."
   * *The Reality*: Requiring phone OTP or account creation adds heavy friction. Storing health data on day one triggers complex **Data Fiduciary** compliance, encryption-at-rest auditing, and consent lifecycle tracking under the DPDP Act 2023.
   * *MVP Decision*: **Stateless / Session-Based Flow**. The user uploads a PDF, receives their vernacular summary and 3 questions in-session, downloads/copies their summary card, and all session data is purged from the server immediately upon completion. Zero stored personal health data = near-zero DPDP compliance risk on Day 1.

2. **Challenged: Smartphone Camera & Paper Photo Ingestion (Moved to LATER)**
   * *The Assumption*: "Many patients have physical printed paper reports."
   * *The Reality*: Camera captures suffer from poor lighting, oblique angles, crumpled paper, and shadows. Failing on OCR recognition damages user trust immediately. In urban and semi-urban India, virtually all labs (Dr. Lal, Apollo, 1mg, SRL, Metropolis) provide an official digital PDF via WhatsApp or SMS.
   * *MVP Decision*: Restrict MVP exclusively to native **Digital PDFs**. This guarantees high parsing accuracy, reliable unit extraction, and zero optical distortion errors.

3. **Challenged: 10+ Regional Indian Languages (Moved to LATER)**
   * *The Assumption*: "We must support all major Indian vernacular languages immediately."
   * *The Reality*: Medical terminology and colloquial phrasing vary significantly across regional dialects. Validating medical translations across 10 languages requires massive clinical QA.
   * *MVP Decision*: Launch MVP strictly with **English + Hindi**. Hindi covers over 50% of the target demographic in North and Central India, allowing clinical translation templates to be thoroughly verified before regional expansion.

4. **Challenged: WhatsApp Bot Interface (Moved to LATER)**
   * *The Assumption*: "Everyone in India uses WhatsApp, so build a WhatsApp bot first."
   * *The Reality*: Setting up WhatsApp Business API requires Meta enterprise verification, message template pre-approvals, per-conversation utility pricing, and complex webhook session management. UI capabilities on WhatsApp for table review or interactive question cards are constrained.
   * *MVP Decision*: Build a responsive, mobile-first **Web Application (PWA)** first. It allows rapid iteration, instant deployment, rich UI for parameter verification, and zero Meta messaging costs.

5. **Challenged: Historical Trend Comparison Graphs (Moved to LATER)**
   * *The Assumption*: "Patients want to see trends over time."
   * *The Reality*: Longitudinal tracking requires user accounts, multi-report storage, and persistent identity mapping. The acute pain point and panic happen *right now* when the latest report arrives.
   * *MVP Decision*: Focus 100% on deciphering the *current* report and generating immediate questions for the upcoming doctor visit.

## 5. Detailed Rationale for "NEVER" (Regulatory & Ethical Guardrails)

The following items are permanently blacklisted from the product roadmap:

1. **Autonomous Disease Diagnosis (NEVER)**
   * *Why*: Telemedicine Practice Guidelines 2020 explicitly state that AI/ML systems are not permitted to counsel patients or independently diagnose medical conditions. Autonomous diagnosis triggers CDSCO Medical Device Rules 2017 (SaMD Class C/D), subjecting the software to clinical trials and regulatory penalties.
   * *Product Rule*: The system will never say *"You have Diabetes"* or *"This indicates kidney failure."* It will say: *"Your Fasting Blood Sugar is 142 mg/dL, which is above the standard reference range of 70–99 mg/dL. Fasting glucose measures the amount of sugar in your bloodstream after overnight fasting. Here are 3 questions to ask your doctor about what this means for you."*

2. **Medication Adjustments & Treatment Suggestions (NEVER)**
   * *Why*: Prescribing or modifying medication dosages without an Registered Medical Practitioner (RMP) is a criminal offense in India under the National Medical Commission Act and Indian Penal Code.
   * *Product Rule*: The product will never advise stopping, starting, or adjusting any pharmaceutical product.

3. **Unverified Home Remedies & Herbal Cures (NEVER)**
   * *Why*: Reddit and community data revealed that the biggest danger of family WhatsApp groups is quackery (e.g., claiming bitter gourd or papaya leaf cures systemic disease). Promoting home remedies violates the Drugs and Magic Remedies (Objectionable Advertisements) Act, 1954.
   * *Product Rule*: The product will never offer or validate informal herbal/folk cures.

4. **Panic-Inducing Disease Probability Scores (NEVER)**
   * *Why*: Symptom checkers failed because showing *"7% chance of Pancreatic Cancer"* triggers acute cyberchondria.
   * *Product Rule*: Never display statistical diagnostic risk percentages or list terrifying differential diagnoses.

5. **Commercial Pharmacy & Aggregator Upselling (NEVER)**
   * *Why*: Consumers despise the "anxiety marketing" of existing aggregators that push unnecessary tests or vitamins based on borderline results.
   * *Product Rule*: ReportSaathi will maintain clean informational neutrality and will never sell affiliate supplements or unneeded diagnostic packages based on lab findings.

6. **Public Cloud LLM Training on User Health Data (NEVER)**
   * *Why*: Uploading identifiable health records to public commercial AI APIs that retain data for model training violates the DPDP Act 2023 and SPDI Rules 2011, exposing the company to statutory penalties up to ₹250 crore.
   * *Product Rule*: All PII is stripped at the client/edge; processing models must operate under zero-data-retention enterprise agreements with data residency strictly within India.
